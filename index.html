What it is

Plain about what it does. Plainer about what it doesn’t.

Beacon is a native Android app (Kotlin, Compose and a local Room database). Everything below is read from its source and README.

Features

Built around how you actually work.

What the v0.1 Android build contains. Each card is a feature that exists in the code.

Plans by energy, not just deadlines

Tasks are sorted as light, medium or deep work. Deep work goes to your sharpest hours and light admin to the dips, once Beacon has enough focus history to know them.

Tells you what didn’t fit

If a task is too long for today, blocked, or out of time, the plan lists it with the reason instead of quietly dropping it.

Slip forecasts with reasons

Every task shows if it’s on track, worth watching or likely to slip, with the cause attached.

Evening review

Commit a plan, then replay the day: finished in its block, late, missed, or done unplanned. Roll what’s left into tomorrow and keep each task’s time of day.

Reminders, widget and overlay

Exact-minute alarms with Snooze and Complete, a home-screen widget, and an optional floating panel to capture tasks over other apps.

Search in plain words

Try “overdue p1” or “deep work this week”. Beacon shows how it read your query, so an empty list never looks like a bug.

Try quick add

Type or say a task the way you’d say it. Beacon picks out the priority, time, repeat, project and tags, and shows them before you tap Add.

How it works

Capture, plan, review. All on the device.

Tasks go into a local database. The planner reads them and your history, places them in your day, and the review compares the plan with what happened.

Privacy & security

Evidence before claims.

No audits, badges or guarantees appear here unless they exist.

Found in the build

  • No internet permission in the manifest, and no analytics or ad SDKs.
  • Optional encrypted backups: AES-256-GCM with a passphrase key.
  • PIN and biometric lock, with limits on PIN attempts.
  • Calendar is read only when you tap “Plan around my calendar”, and only event times.
  • 274 unit tests passed on the last build.

Documented by the owner

  • Backups don’t include alarms, widget settings, the PIN or attachments.
  • After a restore you re-create reminders.
  • Kanban moves use tap buttons, not dragging.
Get started

Three steps, once you have the APK.

Download the APK

Open the latest release on GitHub (v0.1) and download the APK file to your Android phone.

Install

Open the file. Android will ask you to allow installs from that source. Beacon needs Android 8.0 or newer.

Add your first task

Tap +, type a task in plain words, and allow notifications so reminders can appear.

FAQ

Straight answers only.

Does Beacon need an account or internet?

No. The app has no internet permission, so your tasks stay in a database on your phone.

Which version is this?

This is the v0.1 release. It has been tested on the developer’s own Android phones.

What happens to my data if I switch phones?

Export a backup from Settings and restore it on the new phone. Add a passphrase to encrypt it, and keep the passphrase: a lost one can’t be recovered. Alarms aren’t included, so re-create your reminders.

Is it on the Play Store?

Not yet. For now it’s a GitHub release.

Why does it ask for calendar access?

Only if you tap “Plan around my calendar”. Beacon reads event start and end times so your plan skips busy hours.
